## Context and constraints

State the problem, user requirement, uncertainty and decision deadline.

## Options

List viable options and compare operations, cost, privacy, security, performance, licensing and reversibility. Include the option of deferring the capability.

## Decision

State one clear choice and its scope. Mark provisional values and measured facts separately.

## Consequences and risks

Describe what becomes simpler, what becomes harder, failure behavior and residual risk.

## Validation and revisit trigger

Name tests, acceptance thresholds, evidence location and conditions that reopen this decision.

## Sources and linked work

Link primary references, related ADRs and repository issues/PRs when they exist. Never invent issue numbers or approval records.
